## Change things without editing files

- **New Settings page.** Open it from the tray menu to change how the app works. Changes are saved as you make them, and any comments in your `config.toml` are kept.
- **Equalizer:** drag the sliders and watch the bars respond to your music.
- **Players:** add or remove players, one row each. **Detected Right Now** lists the media apps Windows can see, with an **Add** button.
- **Network:** choose who can connect, change the port, and add other names for this PC. If a change needs a restart, a **Restart Now** button appears and brings you back to the page.
- **Start with Windows:** turn it on in Settings or the tray menu.
- **Edits to `config.toml` now apply within a couple of seconds**, without restarting. Only the port and network setting need a Restart. Misspelled setting names are reported on the Settings page and in the log.

## Theme Gallery

- **Filter by feature.** The new **Includes** row finds themes with an equalizer, cover, popup, progress bar, and more. Pick several to see only themes that have all of them.
- **A clearer toolbar** that stays on screen while you scroll.
- **New theme folders appear by themselves.** Add a folder to `static/themes` and it shows up as its own group.
- Favorites are now listed alphabetically.

## More reliable

- **A frozen media app can't hang Now Playing.** If a player stops responding, the overlay keeps showing the last song until it recovers.
- **`localhost` connects right away**, instead of sometimes taking about 2 seconds.
- **Artist names keep their dashes** in Spotify and other players. Only Apple Music's combined "Artist - Album" field is split.
- **`allowed_hosts` entries with a port now work.** 0.2.4 said they did, but they were still refused.
- A failing audio capture is logged once instead of every 2 seconds.
- Themes with impossible timing values no longer break scrolling text or popups.

## Security and Privacy

- **Themes can no longer contact other websites.** A theme from someone else can't use a remote image or font to reveal your IP address or when you're streaming.

**If you use a custom theme that loads fonts or images from the internet,** they will stop loading. Put the files in `static/fonts` or another folder under `static`, and link to them with a path like `/fonts/MyFont/MyFont-Bold.ttf`.
