## More reliable

- **The app tells you if it stops unexpectedly.** If Now Playing ever crashes, or its overlay server stops, a message now says so, instead of the app silently disappearing. The details are in `nowplaying.log` (or `nowplaying.old.log` if you've started it again since).
- **No more leftover tray icon.** After **Quit** or **Restart**, the old icon is removed right away instead of lingering until you move the mouse over it.
- **The cover and your favorites can't be saved half-way.** They're written to a temporary file first, so an overlay loading at the wrong moment, or the app closing mid-save, can't leave a broken cover or lose your favorites. If the cover can't be saved (for example in a read-only folder), the log now says so.
- **The log can't keep growing** if `nowplaying.old.log` is open in another program.

## Settings

- **`allowed_hosts` is more forgiving.** Entries with a port (`desktop-abc.lan:5000`) or a full address (`http://desktop-abc.lan:5000/`) now work, and empty entries are ignored.

## Security and Privacy

- **Each release now comes with a `.sha256` file**, so you can check your download is exactly the published one (see Quick Start in the README).
- **Releases are checked for known security problems** in the libraries the app uses before they're published.
- **A small hardening:** browsers are told not to guess file types.
- **New in the README:** a Privacy section explaining what the app reads, what it saves, and who can connect. THEMES.md now explains what to check before using a theme from someone else.
