## Security and Privacy

- **Other websites can no longer see what you're playing.** Before, a website open in your browser could quietly connect to Now Playing and receive your song changes. Only the app's own pages (the overlay, the gallery, and OBS) can connect now.
- **Only this PC can change settings.** With `bind_address = "0.0.0.0"`, other devices can still view the overlay and gallery, but can't change your favorites.
- **Protection against "DNS rebinding"**, a trick where a website pretends to be your PC.
- **`nowplaying.log` no longer contains your Windows user name or song titles**, so it's safer to paste into a bug report.

Updating is recommended for everyone.
