## Fixes

- **Opening the overlay by the PC's name works again.** 0.2.2 refused addresses like `http://DESKTOP-ABC:5000` from a second PC. The PC's own name (and `name.local`) works again. For other names, such as `desktop-abc.lan`, add them to the new `allowed_hosts` setting in `config.toml`.
- **Live radio and streams now work properly.** Songs without an end time used to count as "not playing": themes dimmed or stopped their animations, and popups didn't appear. They now play normally, with the progress bar and times hidden. Theme authors can style them with `data-live="1"` (see THEMES.md).
- **Times of an hour or more** show as `1:05:30` instead of `65:30`.
- **A typo in a custom theme can no longer freeze the overlay.** Invalid `--marquee-speed` or `--marquee-pause` values now fall back to the defaults.
- **One misbehaving app can no longer blank the overlay.** If another app's media session fails, it's skipped instead of hiding your song.

## Easier troubleshooting

- **The app tells you when it can't start.** If the port is already in use, reserved by Windows, or `bind_address` is wrong, a message explains what to change. Before, the app just never appeared.
- **The previous log is kept** as `nowplaying.old.log`, so starting the app again after it closed unexpectedly no longer erases what happened.
